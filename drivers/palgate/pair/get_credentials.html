How to get your credentials:
1. Run node get-token.js to get your session token
2. Scan the QR code with your PalGate app
3. Copy your phone number, token, and token type
4. Find your Device ID in the PalGate app settings
Your phone number with country code (digits only)
32 hexadecimal characters from get-token.js
Your gate's device ID from PalGate app
Usually "Primary (1)" for Device Linking
